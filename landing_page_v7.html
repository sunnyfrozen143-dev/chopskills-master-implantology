Frequently asked questions

Questions we get every time.

If something isn't here, just send us a WhatsApp — we respond personally.

Do I need prior implant experience to attend?+
No prior implant experience is required. The program is specifically designed for general dentists who have placed zero to a handful of implants. Day 1 covers all the foundational theory and model hands-on work before Day 2 live surgery. If you are an active clinician with a practice, you qualify.
What does "live patient placement" mean exactly?+
On Day 2, a consented patient is seated in the chair and you — the participant — physically place the implant under Dr. Narasimman's direct supervision. You hold the drill. You feel the torque. Dr. Narasimman is standing beside you the entire time. This is not a demo you watch. This is a procedure you do.
What is the "take-home implant"?+
Every Course B participant receives one implant kit — the same system used during the course — to take back to their clinic. The intent is that you use it on your first independent case within days of returning, while the technique is fresh. The in-clinic visit within 5 months is specifically timed to support this first solo case.
Why only 5 seats per batch?+
Because live surgery with individual attention is not scalable. Dr. Narasimman insists on being present for every participant's placement — and that is only possible with an extremely small group. Five is the number where every participant gets real time, real feedback, and real surgical experience. This is not a volume business.
What is the in-clinic visit and when does it happen?+
Within 5 months of your implant placement date at the course, our team visits your clinic — wherever you practice — to guide your first independent implant placement. In-person, step-by-step, in your own setup with your own equipment. There is no extra charge for this. No other implant course in India currently offers this.
What if I can only attend one of the two days?+
Both days are mandatory for Course B participants. Day 1 builds the foundation for Day 2 live surgery — the two days are designed to work together. If you cannot commit to both 4 and 5 July 2026, we would ask you to wait for a future batch where you can attend fully.
What is the difference between Course A and Course B?+
Course A is the foundation track — theory lectures, model hands-on placement, online live Q&A sessions and lifetime mentorship. It does not include live patient placement, the take-home implant, or the in-clinic visit. Course B is the complete program and includes everything. Full details including pricing are sent via WhatsApp when you apply.
